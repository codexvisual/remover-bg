Make better cut-outs: a short guide

Great results start with a good source image. Give your subject space from the background, use even lighting and avoid busy patterns behind them. Then let the AI do its work and finish with the Magic Brush for any stubborn edges.

When you need a consistent look across a catalogue, stick to one background colour and export at the same dimensions every time. Your listings will look deliberate instead of accidental — and that shows in click-through rates.
