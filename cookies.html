Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites can store in your browser. They are commonly used for login sessions, preferences and analytics.

Cookies on this demo

This demo does not set any cookies. There is no login system, no analytics script and no advertising network. Your interaction with the background remover stays in your browser's memory for the duration of the page session.

Third-party resources

The optional AI model file is fetched from a public CDN when you first process an image. That CDN may set its own technical headers, but this site does not control them and does not use them to track you.

Your controls

You can block or clear site data in your browser settings at any time. Doing so will not affect your ability to use the tool.
