Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. What this demo does with your images

By default, this website processes uploaded images entirely inside your browser. The image data is not sent to a server unless you explicitly configure assets/js/config.js to use a server API endpoint. If you configure one, the image is transmitted over HTTPS only to produce your result.

2. Information we collect

The demo forms on this site are illustrative. They display a confirmation message and do not store or transmit the information you enter.

3. Cookies

We do not set tracking or advertising cookies. See the Cookie Policy for details.

4. Third-party resources

The optional AI model is downloaded from a public CDN at the moment you process your first image. That request is made by your browser. No image data is included in the model download.

5. Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data at any time. Since images are held in memory only, reloading the page discards them.

6. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
